Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OmniView is a file viewer that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

What OmniView does not do

  • It does not request the internet permission. The app cannot send data anywhere, because it has no way to connect to a network.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not require an account, sign-up, or login.
  • It does not collect, store, or transmit your files, file names, or any personal information.

What OmniView accesses on your device

To open files, OmniView requests storage access (file access permission). This lets the app read the files you choose to open. Files are read directly from your device's storage and are never copied off the device. Nothing you open in OmniView leaves your phone.

Local data

OmniView keeps a small local database on your device, for its own use only, covering:

  • Your favorited files and recently opened files, so you can find them again.
  • Your app settings, such as light or dark mode.

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. It is never sent anywhere.

The iPhone web app

On iPhone, OmniView runs as a web app in your browser. The files you choose are read in your phone's browser memory to display them, and are never uploaded or sent to any server. The app's own code files are saved on your device so it can open again without internet. The web app has no accounts, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics.

The web app page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may record standard connection details (such as your IP address) when the page loads, as any website host does. That applies to loading the page only, never to the files you open.

Third-party services

OmniView does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact
